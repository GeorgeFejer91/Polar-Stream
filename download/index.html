Vernier Mini signal reference

The Go Direct Respiration Belt (GDX-RB) measures tension in its short strap with a force sensor inside the box. Its documented channels are Force, Respiration Rate, Steps, and Step Rate. No raw X/Y/Z accelerometer channel is documented for this belt. Vernier device manual

Mini outputContentsOrigin
rawVernierAll numeric channels advertised by the connected belt, plus receipt, sequence, drop, and decode diagnostics. Typically Force (N), Respiration Rate (breaths/min), Steps, and Step Rate (steps/min). Values absent from a packet remain NaN.Device channels; diagnostics added by the Mini.
rawForceForce (N) alone, copied from the belt's Force channel. This is respiration effort, not pressure or lung volume.Device Force value; no extra filtering.
vernierBreathingA continuous 0-1 waveform normalized from Force over a rolling 30-second history using the 5th and 95th percentiles after startup. It represents relative belt expansion, not breaths/min.Calculated by Vernier Mini.
signalStatusEvent code 1 when Bluetooth signal is lost; 2 when it is restored. No fake sensor samples are inserted during a gap.Calculated by Vernier Mini.

All four outputs are selected initially. Use Outputs in Vernier Mini to keep any nonempty subset. Separate mode advertises one LSL outlet per selection; Single mode places selected columns in one sparse LSL stream, with NaN where a row does not carry that signal. Changes are saved and applied to active outlets without restarting Bluetooth. The belt's Respiration Rate uses a 30-second window and updates every 10 seconds; Step Rate uses a 10-second window and also updates every 10 seconds. These device values are not the Mini's normalized waveform.

Before first launch

Unsigned research-preview installation

Windows

If SmartScreen appears, choose “More info” and “Run anyway.” The installer is not yet Authenticode-signed.

macOS

Open the DMG and move Polar Stream to Applications. If Gatekeeper blocks it, right-click Open or allow it in Privacy & Security.

Linux

For AppImage, run chmod +x Polar-Stream*.AppImage. Your account needs permission to access the Bluetooth adapter.
